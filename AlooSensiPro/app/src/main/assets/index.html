How to tune after testing

Crosshair stops below target: raise General or Red Dot slightly.
Crosshair goes past target: lower the relevant value slightly.
Scope aim feels shaky: lower that scope value a little and retest.
Change one value at a time. Your HUD button size and thumb movement also affect results.
